1. 我們只支援第三方登入 (OAuth) 與訪客模式

為了保障使用者的帳號密碼安全，HoloHunter 採用現代化的身分驗證機制：

🔒 重要產品決策： 本 App 不提供也不支援 自行註冊 Email 帳號與設定密碼。我們僅支援安全第三方驗證，包含 Google 帳號登入 與 Apple ID 登入。同時我們提供「訪客模式」，無須登入即可閱讀規則與手動查卡。

我們絕對不會要求您輸入、也不會儲存您的任何密碼。身分驗證程序均直接在 Google 或 Apple 的安全防護端進行。

2. 共通帳號、權限模型與多方綁定機制

為了讓您可以在不同裝置及平台上無縫管理您的卡牌資料與訂閱狀態，我們設計了共通帳號系統：

  • 多方綁定：您可以用 Google 登入後再綁定 Apple 帳號，或是用 Apple 登入後再綁定 Google 帳號。
  • 同一識別碼：綁定後，該 Google 與 Apple 帳號將共同對應到同一個內部使用者識別碼 (Internal User ID)，共享相同的卡牌收藏、使用量配額與訂閱權限。
  • 權限模型：我們區分為訪客 (guest)、免費會員 (free_user) 以及付費訂閱會員 (subscriber) 三種角色。免費會員可使用卡牌掃描，但每月上限 100 次；付費訂閱會員享無限掃描，並可解鎖 AI 價格與趨勢預測。訪客模式不支援掃描與價格預測功能。
  • 身分識別原則：我們不使用 Email 作為唯一身分依據（以防 Apple Private Relay 隱藏真實 Email，或不同平台的 Email 不一致）。身分綁定完全基於第三方 Provider 提供的非敏感帳號唯一識別碼。

3. 我們取得的資料與用途

當您登入、綁定或訂閱 Google 或 Apple 帳號時，我們會取得以下有限的資料：

  • 電子郵件地址 (Email)：用於帳號聯絡、客服驗證與通知；不作為唯一登入或帳號合併依據，實際身分綁定以 Google/Apple Provider User ID 為準。
  • 顯示名稱 (Display Name)：用於在 App 介面上提供個人化的歡迎資訊。
  • 第三方帳號識別碼 (Provider User ID)：Google 或 Apple 提供的一串非敏感的唯一識別代碼。
  • 使用量與配額紀錄 (Scan Quota Logs)：安全記錄您在當月所進行的卡牌掃描次數，用於實施免費版每月 100 次的額度控管與防竄改機制。
  • 付費訂閱狀態 (Subscription Status)：記錄您的交易代碼 (Transaction ID / App Store 或 Google Play 憑證) 以開啟付費無限次數與進階預測權限。我們絕不經手且不儲存您的信用卡號等敏感財務資料。

資料用途說明：

  1. 使用者身分驗證與登入：確保您的帳號安全。
  2. 雲端同步服務與訂閱管理：同步您的關注清單、收藏卡牌、每月掃描剩餘額度以及付費訂閱狀態。
  3. 推播通知 (Push Notifications)：若您啟用了卡牌價格提醒功能，我們會使用您的推播 Token（由 Expo Push Service 產生並管理）來發送降價或波動通知。此 Token 僅用於發送通知，不做他用。

相機影像與卡牌辨識處理：

掃描卡牌時，本 App 會使用相機取得卡牌影像進行辨識。影像僅用於辨識卡牌，不會用於廣告或建立個人檔案：

  • 手機 App（iOS / Android）：文字辨識 (OCR) 完全在您的裝置本機進行（expo-ocr-kit），卡牌影像不會離開您的裝置，也不會上傳到我們的伺服器。
  • 網頁版：因瀏覽器無法在本機進行高精度辨識，卡牌影像會傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card，再由第三方 AI 視覺辨識服務（Google Gemini，透過 OpenRouter 中介）即時判讀卡片內容。影像僅用於當次辨識，辨識完成後不作長期儲存，也不會用於廣告或個人化追蹤。

Welcome to HoloHunter ("the App" or "we"). We highly value your personal privacy. This policy describes how we handle multi-provider sign-ins, account linking, subscriptions, and related personal data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

⚠️ Current Build Status (Local Mock / Demo Stage): In the current build, Google / Apple sign-in, account linking, Internal User ID, subscription status, and the monthly scan quota are all a "local mock": signing in merely writes a fixed fake account onto your own device. Any paragraph marked "Future production" is a planned blueprint, not the current state.

1. Third-Party Authentication Only (OAuth) & Guest Mode

To ensure account credential security, HoloHunter employs modern authentication mechanisms:

🔒 Critical Product Decision: The App does not provide or support custom email/password registration. We exclusively support secure third-party login via Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple. We also provide a "Guest Mode" allowing users to read game rules and manually search cards without logging in.

We will never ask for, transmit, or store your passwords. All authentication procedures are performed directly on Google's or Apple's secure servers.

2. Common Account, Permission Model & Multi-Provider Linking

To let you manage card data and subscription states seamlessly across platforms, we use a unified account system:

  • Account Linking: You can log in with Google and then link your Apple account, or log in with Apple and link your Google account.
  • Single Identity ID: Once linked, both Google and Apple identities point to a single Internal User ID, sharing the same card collections, monthly quotas, and subscription state.
  • Permission Model: Users are categorized as guest, free_user, or subscriber. Free users can scan cards up to 100 times per month. Paid subscribers enjoy unlimited scans and access to AI price trend forecasts. Guest mode does not support scanning or forecasts.
  • Identification Principle: We **do NOT use Email as the primary unique key** (to prevent issues with Apple's Private Relay email hiding, or mismatching emails between providers). All linking is tied to the unique provider account ID.

3. Data We Collect and How We Use It

When you sign in, link, or subscribe on HoloHunter, we collect limited profile and usage data:

  • Email Address: Used for account contact, support verification, and notifications. It is not used as the unique login or account-linking key; Google/Apple Provider User ID is the source of truth.
  • Display Name: Used to personalize greetings on the App interface.
  • Provider User ID: A non-sensitive unique ID string provided by Google or Apple.
  • Scan Usage & Quota Logs: Securely logs your monthly scan count to enforce the 100-scan monthly limit for the free tier and prevent local client tampering.
  • Paid Subscription Status: Logs your transaction identifier (Transaction ID / App Store or Google Play receipt metadata) to activate premium features. We **never process or store** your credit card numbers or sensitive financial data.

How Data is Used:

  1. User Authentication: To secure your account and access.
  2. Cloud Syncing & Subscription Management: To sync your watchlists, card collections, monthly quota progress, and premium subscription state.
  3. Push Notifications: If you enable card price alerts, we use your device push token (generated and managed by Expo Push Service) to deliver notifications. This token is used solely for sending alerts.

Camera Images & Card Recognition Processing:

When scanning a card, the App uses the camera to capture the card image for recognition. Images are used only to identify the card, never for advertising or profiling:

  • Mobile App (iOS / Android): Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your device (expo-ocr-kit). The card image never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Web version: Because browsers cannot perform high-accuracy recognition locally, the card image is sent to our server endpoint /api/recognize-card, which uses a third-party AI vision service (Google Gemini, via OpenRouter) to read the card in real time. The image is used only for that single recognition, is not retained long-term, and is never used for advertising or personalized tracking.

  • Tap the Settings tab at the bottom right of the App home screen.
  • In the "Common Account Settings" section, tap the Sign Out button (Guests can sign out to return to onboarding).
