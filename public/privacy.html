Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策
【工程審核草案 / Engineering Review Draft】

最後更新日期：2026 年 8 月 30 日

歡迎使用 HoloHunter（以下簡稱「本 App」或「我們」）。我們非常重視您的個人隱私。本政策說明我們如何處理多方帳號登入、綁定、訂閱狀態與相關個人資料。當您使用本 App 時，即表示您同意本政策。

目前版本狀態： Google 帳號登入與雲端帳號已實際運作：登入會向 Google 取得您的 ID Token，並在我們的後端（Vercel KV）建立一筆雲端帳號紀錄。收藏、牌組、價格提醒與設定會在您登入時同步到該帳號。因此確實存在儲存於雲端、可被刪除的個人資料，刪除方式見第 5 節。

尚未實作的部分：本 App 目前沒有任何付費或訂閱機制——沒有串接 App Store、Google Play Billing 或任何第三方金流，不會處理付款、不會收費，也不會取得或儲存任何交易代碼與財務資料。App 內亦未啟用任何廣告或分析追蹤 SDK。Android 版的 Apple ID 登入預設為關閉。凡下方標示為「未來正式版」的段落皆為規劃藍圖，並非現況。

1. 我們只支援第三方登入 (OAuth) 與訪客模式

為了保障使用者的帳號密碼安全，HoloHunter 採用現代化的身分驗證機制：

🔒 重要產品決策： 本 App 不提供也不支援 自行註冊 Email 帳號與設定密碼。我們僅支援安全第三方驗證，包含 Google 帳號登入 與 Apple ID 登入。同時我們提供「訪客模式」，無須登入即可閱讀規則與手動查卡。

我們絕對不會要求您輸入、也不會儲存您的任何密碼。身分驗證程序均直接在 Google 或 Apple 的安全防護端進行。

2. 共通帳號、權限模型與多方綁定機制

為了讓您可以在不同裝置及平台上無縫管理您的卡牌資料與訂閱狀態，我們設計了共通帳號系統：

  • 多方綁定：您可以用 Google 登入後再綁定 Apple 帳號，或是用 Apple 登入後再綁定 Google 帳號。
  • 同一識別碼：綁定後，該 Google 與 Apple 帳號將共同對應到同一個內部使用者識別碼 (Internal User ID)，共享相同的卡牌收藏、使用量配額與訂閱權限。
  • 權限模型：我們區分為訪客 (guest)、免費會員 (free_user) 以及付費訂閱會員 (subscriber) 三種角色。免費會員可使用卡牌掃描，但每月上限 100 次；付費訂閱會員享無限掃描，並可解鎖 AI 價格與趨勢預測。訪客模式不支援掃描與價格預測功能。
  • 身分識別原則：我們不使用 Email 作為唯一身分依據（以防 Apple Private Relay 隱藏真實 Email，或不同平台的 Email 不一致）。身分綁定完全基於第三方 Provider 提供的非敏感帳號唯一識別碼。

3. 我們取得的資料與用途

當您登入、綁定或訂閱 Google 或 Apple 帳號時，我們會取得以下有限的資料：

  • 電子郵件地址 (Email)：用於帳號聯絡、客服驗證與通知；不作為唯一登入或帳號合併依據，實際身分綁定以 Google/Apple Provider User ID 為準。
  • 顯示名稱 (Display Name)：用於在 App 介面上提供個人化的歡迎資訊。
  • 第三方帳號識別碼 (Provider User ID)：Google 或 Apple 提供的一串非敏感的唯一識別代碼。
  • 使用量與配額紀錄 (Scan Quota Logs)：安全記錄您在當月所進行的卡牌掃描次數，用於實施免費版每月 100 次的額度控管與防竄改機制。
  • 付費訂閱狀態 (Subscription Status)：目前未實作。本 App 尚未串接任何金流，不會取得、傳輸或儲存交易代碼、購買紀錄、信用卡號或任何其他財務資料。此欄位為未來正式版的規劃，屆時將另行更新本政策。

資料用途說明：

  1. 使用者身分驗證與登入：確保您的帳號安全。
  2. 雲端同步服務與訂閱管理：同步您的關注清單、收藏卡牌、每月掃描剩餘額度以及付費訂閱狀態。
  3. 推播通知 (Push Notifications)：若您啟用了卡牌價格提醒功能，我們會使用您的推播 Token（由 Expo Push Service 產生並管理）來發送降價或波動通知。此 Token 僅用於發送通知，不做他用。

相機影像與卡牌辨識處理：

掃描卡牌時，本 App 會使用相機取得卡牌影像進行辨識。影像僅用於辨識卡牌，不會用於廣告或建立個人檔案：

  • 手機 App（iOS / Android）與網頁版皆會上傳影像：不論您是用相機拍攝或從相簿選取，卡牌影像都會傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card，再由第三方 AI 視覺辨識服務 Google Gemini（Google AI Studio 的 Generative Language API，直接呼叫、無中介轉送）即時判讀卡片內容。
  • 保存方式：我們的伺服器不會儲存該影像——它只在該次請求的記憶體中處理並轉送，請求結束即不復存在。影像僅用於當次辨識，不會用於廣告、轉售或建立個人檔案。Google 對透過 Generative Language API 傳送之內容的保存與使用，適用 Google 的條款。
  • 裝置端 OCR 僅為備援：當上述伺服器辨識失敗或無法連線時，App 會退回使用裝置本機的文字辨識（expo-ocr-kit）。這是備援路徑，不是預設路徑。

4. 使用者如何登出

您可以隨時結束您在 App 中的登入狀態：

  • 點擊 App 首頁右下角的「設定 (Settings)」分頁。
  • 在「共通帳號設定」區段中，點擊「登出 (Sign Out)」按鈕（若為訪客則可登出回到登入頁面）。
  • 登出後，App 將立即清除您在本機裝置上的登入暫存快取。

5. 帳號與資料刪除方式

App 內刪除帳號（已實作）：

進入「設定」分頁，點擊「刪除帳號與資料」並確認後，App 會呼叫我們的後端刪除端點，級聯刪除下列雲端資料，並清除本機 Session 與快取後將您登出。此為不可逆操作：

  1. 您的雲端帳號紀錄（Internal User ID、顯示名稱、Email、頭像網址、角色）。
  2. 所有已綁定的第三方身分對應（Google / Apple 的 Provider User ID 與其索引）。
  3. 雲端同步資料（收藏、牌組、卡牌收藏數量、價格提醒、設定）。

需以電子郵件申請刪除的部分：

推播 Token 不會被上述刪除流程移除。 若您曾允許推播通知，您裝置的推播 Token 會保存在我們的後端，且目前沒有自助解除註冊的機制；刪除帳號或移除 App 都不會刪除它。如需刪除，請將刪除申請寄至 dicoge.chen@gmail.com，我們會人工移除並回信通知您。

若您在 App 內無法完成刪除（例如已解除安裝），亦可寄信至上述地址並附上您登入所用的 Email 或 Internal User ID，由我們代為執行相同的級聯刪除。

Welcome to HoloHunter ("the App" or "we"). We highly value your personal privacy. This policy describes how we handle multi-provider sign-ins, account linking, subscriptions, and related personal data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Current Build Status: Google Sign-In and cloud accounts are live. Signing in obtains an ID token from Google and creates a cloud account record in our backend (Vercel KV). Your favorites, decks, price alerts, and settings sync to that account while you are signed in. There is therefore personal data stored in the cloud that can be deleted — see Section 5.

Not implemented: the App has no payment or subscription mechanism at all. It is not connected to the App Store, Google Play Billing, or any third-party payment processor; it does not process payments, does not charge you, and does not obtain or store any transaction identifier or financial data. No advertising or analytics tracking SDK is enabled. Sign in with Apple is disabled by default on Android. Any paragraph below marked "Future production" is a planned blueprint, not the current state.

1. Third-Party Authentication Only (OAuth) & Guest Mode

To ensure account credential security, HoloHunter employs modern authentication mechanisms:

🔒 Critical Product Decision: The App does not provide or support custom email/password registration. We exclusively support secure third-party login via Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple. We also provide a "Guest Mode" allowing users to read game rules and manually search cards without logging in.

We will never ask for, transmit, or store your passwords. All authentication procedures are performed directly on Google's or Apple's secure servers.

2. Common Account, Permission Model & Multi-Provider Linking

To let you manage card data and subscription states seamlessly across platforms, we use a unified account system:

  • Account Linking: You can log in with Google and then link your Apple account, or log in with Apple and link your Google account.
  • Single Identity ID: Once linked, both Google and Apple identities point to a single Internal User ID, sharing the same card collections, monthly quotas, and subscription state.
  • Permission Model: Users are categorized as guest, free_user, or subscriber. Free users can scan cards up to 100 times per month. Paid subscribers enjoy unlimited scans and access to AI price trend forecasts. Guest mode does not support scanning or forecasts.
  • Identification Principle: We **do NOT use Email as the primary unique key** (to prevent issues with Apple's Private Relay email hiding, or mismatching emails between providers). All linking is tied to the unique provider account ID.

3. Data We Collect and How We Use It

When you sign in, link, or subscribe on HoloHunter, we collect limited profile and usage data:

  • Email Address: Used for account contact, support verification, and notifications. It is not used as the unique login or account-linking key; Google/Apple Provider User ID is the source of truth.
  • Display Name: Used to personalize greetings on the App interface.
  • Provider User ID: A non-sensitive unique ID string provided by Google or Apple.
  • Scan Usage & Quota Logs: Securely logs your monthly scan count to enforce the 100-scan monthly limit for the free tier and prevent local client tampering.
  • Paid Subscription Status: Not implemented. The App is not connected to any payment processor and does not obtain, transmit, or store transaction identifiers, purchase history, credit card numbers, or any other financial data. This field describes a future production plan; we will update this policy before any of it takes effect.

How Data is Used:

  1. User Authentication: To secure your account and access.
  2. Cloud Syncing & Subscription Management: To sync your watchlists, card collections, monthly quota progress, and premium subscription state.
  3. Push Notifications: If you enable card price alerts, we use your device push token (generated and managed by Expo Push Service) to deliver notifications. This token is used solely for sending alerts.

Camera Images & Card Recognition Processing:

When scanning a card, the App uses the camera to capture the card image for recognition. Images are used only to identify the card, never for advertising or profiling:

  • The image is uploaded on mobile (iOS / Android) as well as on the web: whether you capture it with the camera or select it from your photo library, the card image is sent to our server endpoint /api/recognize-card, which calls the third-party AI vision service Google Gemini (Google AI Studio's Generative Language API, called directly with no intermediary router) to read the card in real time.
  • Retention: our server does not store the image — it is held only in memory for the duration of that request and forwarded, and does not exist once the request ends. The image is used solely for that single recognition and never for advertising, resale, or profiling. Google's own terms govern its retention and use of content sent to the Generative Language API.
  • On-device OCR is only a fallback: if the server recognition fails or is unreachable, the App falls back to on-device text recognition (expo-ocr-kit). This is a fallback path, not the default one.

4. How Users Sign Out

You can sign out of the App at any time:

  • Tap the Settings tab at the bottom right of the App home screen.
  • In the "Common Account Settings" section, tap the Sign Out button (Guests can sign out to return to onboarding).
  • Upon signing out, cached user profile credentials on your local device will be cleared immediately.

5. Account & Data Deletion

In-app account deletion (implemented):

Open the Settings tab, tap Delete Account and confirm. The App calls our backend deletion endpoint, which cascade-deletes the cloud data below, then clears your local session and cache and signs you out. The action is irreversible:

  1. Your cloud account record (Internal User ID, display name, email, avatar URL, role).
  2. Every linked third-party identity mapping (the Google / Apple Provider User IDs and their indexes).
  3. Cloud-synced data (favorites, decks, card collection counts, price alerts, settings).

What still requires an email request:

The push token is not removed by the deletion flow above. If you ever allowed push notifications, your device's push token is retained in our backend, and there is currently no self-service way to unregister it; neither deleting your account nor uninstalling the App deletes it. To have it removed, email a deletion request to dicoge.chen@gmail.com and we will remove it manually and confirm by reply.

If you cannot complete deletion inside the App (for example, you already uninstalled it), email the same address with the email address you signed in with or your Internal User ID, and we will run the same cascade deletion on your behalf.

The following store-compliance items apply after the App integrates real billing and a backend; the current mock build does not yet implement real subscriptions or a deletion backend.

  • App Store Account Deletion & Subscriptions: the in-app account deletion entry point is already available in Settings and is wired to the backend (see Section 5), with an email channel for items that cannot be self-served, such as the push token. Subscriptions have not shipped; if they ever do, the Android build will use Google Play Billing in line with Google Play policy and will be cancellable from your store account settings.
  • Google Play Data Safety:
    • Transit Security: All data collected is transmitted using secure encryption protocols (HTTPS) to prevent interception.

    • Functional Third-Party Processing: The only exception is card recognition — on both the mobile App and the web version, the card image is sent in real time to the AI vision service Google Gemini (Google AI Studio's Generative Language API, called directly with no intermediary router) for processing. This is solely to provide the recognition feature, never for advertising, resale, or profiling.
