ℹ️ 資料儲存重點： 本 App 採用伺服器權威 (server-authoritative) 的帳號系統。當您登入後，我們的後端（託管於 Vercel，身分資料存放於 Vercel KV）會為您建立並儲存一個內部帳號，並發給您的裝置一組登入 session。相對地，您的收藏、追蹤清單、語言／幣別偏好與每月掃描次數計數僅儲存在您自己的裝置本機（手機 App 為 AsyncStorage、網頁版為瀏覽器 localStorage），不會上傳到伺服器，也不會在裝置之間同步。會離開您裝置的資料為：(1) 登入時送交伺服器驗證與儲存的帳號身分資料，(2) 掃描卡牌時上傳的卡片影像，(3) 若您在手機上開啟通知，會上傳的推播 Token。詳見下方各節。

1. 登入方式：Google／Apple 登入與訪客模式

本 App 不提供自建 Email／密碼註冊，也不會要求、傳輸或儲存您的任何密碼，也不會在您的裝置上儲存 Google／Apple 的 OAuth access／refresh token。登入時，App 只在您的裝置上向登入服務商取得一組短效的 ID Token，交由我們的後端驗證；身分的解析與帳號的擁有權都由伺服器負責（server-authoritative）。目前提供的使用方式：

  • Google 帳號登入（已啟用）：採 Google 官方 OAuth 2.0 / OIDC。網頁版走 PKCE、iOS 走原生 Google OAuth client；驗證由 Google 完成，最終身分由我們的後端解析。
  • Sign in with Apple（iOS 已啟用）：在 iOS 上以系統原生的 Sign in with Apple 進行，App 會將 Apple 身分權杖交由後端驗證。網頁版的 Apple 登入則由伺服器端功能旗標控管（需 Services ID 與後端密鑰）；未開啟時，Apple 按鈕會完全隱藏，不會顯示為無法使用的按鈕。
  • 綁定多個登入方式：您可將 Google 與 Apple 綁定至同一個帳號，也可以解除綁定（但需至少保留一種登入方式）。
  • 訪客模式：無須登入即可瀏覽卡表與手動查價。（相機掃描辨識功能需登入後才能使用。）

2. 登入時我們取得並儲存哪些資料，儲存在哪裡

當您登入時，我們的後端會在伺服器端（Vercel KV 身分資料庫）為您建立並儲存一個內部帳號，其中包含：

  • 內部使用者識別碼：本 App 自行產生的帳號主鍵。
  • 登入服務商的識別碼 (provider subject)：來自 Google／Apple 的穩定識別碼。帳號身分以 (登入服務商, subject) 為鍵，不以 Email 作為身分依據——Email 僅為快照，不參與身分解析或帳號合併（因此 Apple 私密轉寄或變更 Email 不會造成錯誤合併）。
  • 電子郵件、顯示名稱、個人資料相片網址：向登入服務商取得的快照，儲存於伺服器帳號與各綁定身分紀錄中，用於在介面顯示。
  • 帳號角色 (role) 與綁定時間：例如免費／訂閱者狀態、各登入方式的綁定時間戳。

您的裝置會快取一份上述帳號快照與 session 權杖（手機 AsyncStorage／網頁 localStorage）以維持登入；每次啟動時 session 會向伺服器重新驗證，伺服器永遠是資料的權威來源。以上資料僅用於：讓您登入、在介面顯示帳號資訊、維持登入狀態與帳號管理。掃描功能設有每月次數上限（登入的免費使用者每月 100 次），此計數僅儲存在您的裝置本機、非伺服器端配額，訪客則無法使用掃描。本 App 目前沒有可購買的付費訂閱、也不處理任何付款；如未來導入，將於本政策另行揭露。

3. 相機影像與卡牌辨識（會上傳到伺服器並交由 Google Gemini 辨識）

使用掃描功能時，本 App 會以相機取得卡牌影像來辨識卡片。請注意目前的實際流程：

📷 手機 App（iOS / Android）與網頁版皆會上傳影像。 兩個平台在掃描時都會將擷取並前處理後的卡牌影像，傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card；伺服器再將影像轉送給 Google Gemini 視覺辨識模型（Google 的 Generative Language API，generativelanguage.googleapis.com）進行即時判讀。

  • 用途：影像僅用於辨識該張卡片以提供查價，不會用於廣告或建立個人檔案。
  • 內容：影像為卡片畫面，可能含背景，不含您的帳號或身分識別資訊。
  • 保留：本 App 伺服器端不長期儲存影像；影像於傳輸期間交由 Google 依其政策處理。Google 隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. 推播通知與追蹤清單

  • 追蹤清單 (Watchlist)：您加入追蹤的卡牌只儲存在裝置本機，不會上傳到伺服器。
  • 推播 Token（僅手機 App）：若您在手機上允許通知權限，App 會取得一組 Expo 推播 Token，連同平台別（iOS / Android）上傳到我們的伺服器並存放於 Vercel KV。此 Token 以裝置為索引、不與您的 Google 帳號綁定，僅用於未來發送卡牌價格通知。網頁版不會取得或上傳推播 Token。

5. 如何登出

您可以隨時在 App 首頁的「設定」分頁點擊「登出」。登出僅會清除儲存在您裝置上的 session 與帳號快照，讓您在本裝置登出。登出不會向 Google 或 Apple 撤銷授權，也不會刪除伺服器端的帳號資料。若您想在登入服務商端撤銷本 App 的存取權，請前往 Google 帳戶 → 安全性 → 第三方應用程式與服務，或 Apple ID → 使用 Apple 登入 移除 HoloHunter。

ℹ️ 「刪除帳號」為伺服器權威、fail-closed 的刪除。 在「設定」分頁點擊「刪除帳號」並確認後，App 會呼叫後端執行刪除；只有在伺服器確認成功時，才會將您的內部帳號與所有綁定身分（Google／Apple 的 subject、Email／名稱／頭像快照等）從我們的資料庫（Vercel KV）永久刪除，並清除本裝置的 session。若伺服器未能確認刪除，App 不會宣稱已刪除，您仍為登入狀態，可稍後再試或聯絡我們。

Welcome to HoloHunter ("the App" or "we"). HoloHunter is an unofficial price-lookup tool for players of the hololive OFFICIAL CARD GAME (hOCG). This policy describes what data the App actually collects today, how it is used and stored, which third parties it talks to, and how you can sign out and delete data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

ℹ️ Where your data lives: HoloHunter uses a server-authoritative account system. When you sign in, our backend (hosted on Vercel, with identity data in Vercel KV) creates and stores an internal account for you and issues a session to your device. By contrast, your collections, watchlist, language/currency preferences, and monthly scan counter are stored only on your own device (AsyncStorage on the mobile App, browser localStorage on the web); they are not uploaded to our servers and do not sync across devices. The data that leaves your device is: (1) the account/identity data sent to our server for verification and storage at sign-in, (2) the card image uploaded when you scan a card, and (3) if you enable notifications on the mobile App, your push token. See the sections below.

1. Sign-In: Google / Apple Sign-In & Guest Mode

The App does not offer custom email/password registration, never asks for, transmits, or stores any password, and does not store Google/Apple OAuth access or refresh tokens on your device. At sign-in, the App only obtains a short-lived provider ID token on your device and sends it to our backend for verification; identity resolution and account ownership are handled on the server (server-authoritative). The available ways to use it:

  • Google Sign-In (enabled): Uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 / OIDC — PKCE on the web, a native Google OAuth client on iOS. Google performs authentication; the final identity is resolved by our backend.
  • Sign in with Apple (enabled on iOS): On iOS this uses the system-native Sign in with Apple, and the App forwards the Apple identity token to our backend for verification. Web Apple sign-in is gated behind a server-side feature flag (it requires a Services ID and a backend secret); while off, the Apple button is hidden entirely rather than shown as a non-functional control.
  • Linking multiple methods: You may link both Google and Apple to the same account, and unlink them (as long as at least one login method remains).
  • Guest Mode: Browse the card list and look up prices manually without signing in. (The camera scan recognition feature requires signing in.)

2. What We Collect and Store at Sign-In, and Where

When you sign in, our backend creates and stores an internal account for you in the server-side identity database (Vercel KV), containing:

  • Internal user ID: The App's own account primary key.
  • Provider subject: The stable identifier from Google/Apple. Account identity is keyed by (provider, subject), not by email — email is only a snapshot and never participates in identity resolution or account merging (so Apple private-relay or a changed email cannot cause a wrong merge).
  • Email, display name, and profile picture URL: Snapshots obtained from the provider, stored on the server account and on each linked identity record, used to display your account in the UI.
  • Account role and link timestamps: e.g. free/subscriber status and the timestamp each login method was linked.

Your device caches a copy of that account snapshot plus the session token (AsyncStorage on mobile / localStorage on web) to keep you signed in; on each launch the session is re-validated against the server, and the server is always the source of truth. This data is used solely to sign you in, display your account info in the UI, keep you signed in, and manage your account. The scan feature has a monthly usage limit (100 scans/month for signed-in free users); this count is stored only on your device and is not a server-side quota, and guests cannot scan. The App currently has no purchasable paid subscription and processes no payments; if any are introduced later, this policy will be updated to disclose them.

3. Camera Images & Card Recognition (uploaded and processed by Google Gemini)

When you use the scan feature, the App uses the camera to capture a card image for recognition. Please note the actual flow today:

📷 Both the mobile App (iOS / Android) and the web version upload the image. On both platforms, the captured and pre-processed card image is sent to our server endpoint /api/recognize-card, which then forwards it to Google's Gemini vision model (Google's Generative Language API, generativelanguage.googleapis.com) for real-time reading.

  • Purpose: The image is used only to identify that card for price lookup, never for advertising or profiling.
  • Contents: The image is a picture of the card and may include background; it carries no account or identity information.
  • Retention: Our server does not retain the image long-term; during transit it is processed by Google under its policy. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Push Notifications & Watchlist

  • Watchlist: Cards you add to your watchlist are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to the server.
  • Push token (mobile App only): If you grant notification permission on the mobile App, it obtains an Expo push token and uploads it, together with the platform (iOS / Android), to our server, where it is stored in Vercel KV. The token is keyed by device and is not linked to your Google account; it is only for delivering future card price notifications. The web version does not obtain or upload a push token.

5. How to Sign Out

You can sign out at any time from the Settings tab by tapping Sign Out. Signing out only clears the session and account snapshot stored on your device, signing you out on this device. It does not revoke your Google or Apple authorization, and does not delete your server-side account data. To revoke the App's access at the provider, go to your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services, or Apple ID → Sign in with Apple, and remove HoloHunter.

6. Account & Data Deletion

ℹ️ "Delete Account" is a server-authoritative, fail-closed deletion. When you tap Delete Account in Settings and confirm, the App calls the backend to perform the deletion. Only when the server confirms success does it permanently delete your internal account and all linked identities (the Google/Apple subjects and the email/name/photo snapshots) from our database (Vercel KV) and clear the session on this device. If the server cannot confirm deletion, the App does not claim it was deleted — you remain signed in and can retry later or contact us.

Also note:

  1. Apple authorization revocation: Per App Store guideline 5.1.1(v), if your account has a linked Apple identity, deletion first requires the server to successfully revoke your Apple authorization; if revocation cannot be completed, the deletion is refused and nothing is deleted (fail-closed). (Note: persistence for Apple-token revocation is still being finalized, so accounts with a linked Apple identity may currently receive a "not yet available" result and are intentionally not deleted until it ships.)
  2. Deletion scope: Account deletion covers your server-side account/identity data. It does not cover: the anonymous push token (email us to remove — see below), or your device-local data (collections, watchlist, preferences, scan history).
  3. Lawful-retention exception (narrow): As a rule, once the server confirms deletion we retain none of your account/identity data. We retain data only where applicable law explicitly requires it, and then only the minimum data necessary for that legal obligation, only for the legally mandated period, used solely to meet that obligation, and deleted once the period ends. The App currently has no payment or transaction features, so there are no payment/transaction records to retain today; this exception does not extend to retention for general operational or business purposes.
  4. Delete an uploaded push token: There is no in-app removal entry yet; to delete it, email dicoge.chen@gmail.com and we will remove it on the server.
  5. Clear local data: Uninstalling the App (mobile) or clearing site data (web) removes all locally stored collections, watchlist, and settings.
