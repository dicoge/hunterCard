目前版本狀態： Google 帳號登入與雲端帳號已實際運作：登入會向 Google 取得您的 ID Token，並在我們的後端（Vercel KV）建立一筆雲端帳號紀錄。目前 Store MVP 上架版本不會啟動雲端同步 binding（App 內 installAccountSyncBinding() 由 FEATURES.favorites || FEATURES.watchlist || FEATURES.premium 控管，Store MVP 下三個 flag 全部為 false），因此收藏、牌組、價格提醒與設定不會在 Production 版本上被送往 /api/auth/sync；此欄位僅在 Web Develop／Staging 或未來 feature flag 打開的建構中同步。無論如何，帳號基本資料（Internal User ID、Email、顯示名稱、Provider 綁定）仍然是儲存於雲端、可被刪除的個人資料，刪除方式見第 5 節。

為了保障使用者的帳號密碼安全，HoloHunter 採用現代化的身分驗證機制：

🔒 重要產品決策： 本 App 不提供也不支援 自行註冊 Email 帳號與設定密碼。我們僅支援安全第三方驗證，包含 Google 帳號登入 與 Apple ID 登入。同時我們提供「訪客模式」，無須登入即可閱讀規則與手動查卡。

2. 共通帳號、權限模型與多方綁定機制

為了讓您可以在不同裝置及平台上無縫管理您的卡牌資料與訂閱狀態，我們設計了共通帳號系統：

  • 多方綁定：您可以用 Google 登入後再綁定 Apple 帳號，或是用 Apple 登入後再綁定 Google 帳號。
  • 同一識別碼：綁定後，該 Google 與 Apple 帳號將共同對應到同一個內部使用者識別碼 (Internal User ID)。每月掃描配額目前為裝置端計數（同一 Internal User ID 不會跨裝置合計），付費訂閱權限目前尚未實作；兩者會隨伺服器端 quota 與 App 內購上線時再納入 Internal User ID 的共享範圍。
  • 權限模型：目前 App 只提供訪客 (guest) 與免費會員 (free_user) 兩種角色。免費會員可使用卡牌掃描，每月上限 100 次（與 App 內 MONTHLY_SCAN_LIMIT 一致）；訪客模式不支援掃描與價格預測功能。付費訂閱會員 (subscriber) 為未來版本規劃，需等 App 內購與金流上線後才會啟用；在啟用之前，App 內部會把任何 subscriber 角色狀態強制回退為 free_user，因此不會出現任何裝置端偽造解鎖的路徑。
  • 身分識別原則：我們不使用 Email 作為唯一身分依據（以防 Apple Private Relay 隱藏真實 Email，或不同平台的 Email 不一致）。身分綁定完全基於第三方 Provider 提供的非敏感帳號唯一識別碼。

相機影像與卡牌辨識處理：

掃描卡牌時，本 App 會使用相機取得卡牌影像進行辨識。影像僅用於辨識卡牌，不會用於廣告或建立個人檔案：

  • 手機 App（iOS / Android）：不論您是用相機拍攝或從相簿選取，卡牌影像都不會離開您的裝置。辨識由裝置本機的文字辨識（expo-ocr-kit）完成，影像不會上傳到我們的伺服器，也不會傳送給任何第三方 AI 服務。
  • 拍攝的照片會寫入 App 私有快取：相機拍照後，照片會存放在 App 專屬的裝置內快取目錄，供辨識與預覽使用。App 不會主動清除這些快取檔案；解除安裝 App 時系統會一併移除。
  • 網頁版：因瀏覽器無法在本機進行高精度辨識，網頁版的卡牌影像會傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card，再由第三方 AI 視覺辨識服務 Google Gemini（Google AI Studio 的 Generative Language API，直接呼叫、無中介轉送）即時判讀。我們的伺服器不會儲存該影像——它只在該次請求中轉送，請求結束即不復存在；影像僅用於當次辨識，不會用於廣告、轉售或建立個人檔案。

1. Third-Party Authentication Only (OAuth) & Guest Mode

2. Common Account, Permission Model & Multi-Provider Linking

To let you manage card data and subscription states seamlessly across platforms, we use a unified account system:

Camera Images & Card Recognition Processing:

When scanning a card, the App uses the camera to capture the card image for recognition. Images are used only to identify the card, never for advertising or profiling:

  • Mobile App (iOS / Android): whether you capture the card with the camera or select it from your photo library, the card image never leaves your device. Our server does not store the image — it is only forwarded within that request and does not exist once it ends. The image is used solely for that single recognition and never for advertising, resale, or profiling.

5. Account & Data Deletion

In-app account deletion — depends on how you signed in:

Accounts linked with Google (in-app self-service is available): open the Settings tab, tap Delete Account and confirm. The App calls our backend deletion endpoint (POST /api/auth/delete-account), which cascade-deletes the cloud data below, then clears your local session and cache and signs you out. The action is irreversible. On a successful delete, the App calls GoogleSignin.signOut() on Android to clear the local Google SDK cache; this does NOT revoke your Google refresh_token server-side (your Google account itself is not revoked — we simply no longer hold your identity mapping). iOS / web currently have no analogous SDK-side clear, and those platforms do not retain a Google SDK session to begin with.

  1. Your cloud account record (Internal User ID, display name, email, avatar URL, role).
  2. Every linked Google identity mapping (Google Provider User IDs and their indexes).
  3. Cloud-synced data if any was written (favorites, decks, card collection counts, price alerts, settings on /api/auth/sync). The Store MVP build does not install the sync binding, so Production currently has no such data to cascade-delete; this item covers data written by feature-flag-enabled builds (Web Develop / Staging).

Accounts linked with Apple (in-app deletion is NOT available yet — email required): Apple's platform-deletion contract requires the backend to revoke the refresh_token issued at sign-in. That requires encrypted server-side storage of the refresh_token; today api/_lib/apple-token-store.ts is a non-shipping stub — persistAppleRefreshToken throws and getStoredAppleRefreshToken always returns null. Consequently POST /api/auth/delete-account fails closed with 501 apple_deletion_not_implemented for Apple-linked accounts and does not delete anything (so we never leave an Apple identity mapping we cannot revoke). Apple-linked users: please email dicoge.chen@gmail.com with the email you signed in with or your Internal User ID, and we will run the same cascade deletion by hand via the email channel described below. This policy and the in-app deletion entry point will be updated together when encrypted Apple refresh_token storage ships.

What still requires an email request:
