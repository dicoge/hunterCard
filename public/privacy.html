歡迎使用 HoloHunter（以下簡稱「本 App」或「我們」）。我們非常重視您的個人隱私。本政策說明我們如何處理多方帳號登入、綁定及相關個人資料。當您使用本 App 時，即表示您同意本政策。

1. 我們只支援第三方登入 (OAuth)

為了保障使用者的帳號密碼安全，HoloHunter 採用現代化的身分驗證機制：

🔒 重要產品決策： 本 App 不提供也不支援 自行註冊 Email 帳號與設定密碼。我們僅支援安全第三方驗證，包含 Google 帳號登入 與 Apple ID 登入。Web 網頁版亦提供 Google 登入支援。

我們絕對不會要求您輸入、也不會儲存您的任何密碼。身分驗證程序均直接在 Google 或 Apple 的安全防護端進行。

2. 共通帳號與多方綁定機制

為了讓您可以在不同裝置及平台上無縫管理您的卡牌資料，我們設計了共通帳號系統：

  • 多方綁定：您可以用 Google 登入後再綁定 Apple 帳號，或是用 Apple 登入後再綁定 Google 帳號。
  • 同一識別碼：綁定後，該 Google 與 Apple 帳號將共同對應到同一個內部使用者識別碼 (Internal User ID)，共享相同的卡牌收藏、入手提醒 (Watchlist) 與偏好設定。
  • 解除綁定安全限制：您隨時可以解除綁定其中一個帳號，但為了防止帳號遺失，系統規定必須保留至少一個登入方式。
  • 身分識別原則：我們不使用 Email 作為唯一身分依據（以防 Apple Private Relay 隱藏真實 Email，或不同平台的 Email 不一致）。身分綁定完全基於第三方 Provider 提供的非敏感帳號唯一識別碼。

3. 我們取得的資料與用途

當您登入或綁定 Google 或 Apple 帳號時，我們會取得以下有限的公開資料：

  • 電子郵件地址 (Email)：作為您的唯一帳號識別與通訊之用（可能為 Private Relay 匿名郵箱）。
  • 顯示名稱 (Display Name)：用於在 App 介面上提供個人化的歡迎資訊。
  • 第三方帳號識別碼 (Provider User ID)：Google 或 Apple 提供的一串非敏感的唯一識別代碼。

資料用途說明：

  1. 使用者身分驗證與登入：確保您的帳號安全。
  2. 雲端同步服務：同步您的關注清單、收藏卡牌以及個人偏好設定，讓您在多個裝置或網頁版之間無縫切換。
  3. 推播通知 (Push Notifications)：若您啟用了卡牌價格提醒功能，我們會使用您的推播 Token（由 Expo Push Service 產生並管理）來發送降價或波動通知。此 Token 僅用於發送通知，不做他用。

4. 使用者如何登出

您可以隨時結束您在 App 中的登入狀態：

  • 點擊 App 首頁右下角的「設定 (Settings)」分頁。
  • 在「共通帳號設定」區段中，點擊「登出 (Sign Out)」按鈕。
  • 登出後，App 將立即清除您在本機裝置上的登入暫存快取。

符合 Apple App Store 與 Google Play 的最新合規規範，我們提供完善的「被遺忘權」與資料刪除機制：

⚠️ 注意： 刪除帳號是不可逆的操作。刪除後，您的 Internal User ID、所有綁定的 Google/Apple 第三方帳號連結 (Linked Providers) 以及雲端的所有收藏清單、偏好與資料將被永久抹除，無法還原。

申請刪除管道：

  1. App 內自主刪除（推薦）：進入「設定」分頁，點擊紅色的「刪除帳號與資料 (Delete Account)」按鈕，並於彈出的對話框中確認。系統將立即且永久刪除您在資料庫中的所有關聯資料。

Welcome to HoloHunter ("the App" or "we"). We highly value your personal privacy. This policy describes how we handle multi-provider sign-ins, account linking, and related personal data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Third-Party Authentication Only (OAuth)

To ensure account credential security, HoloHunter employs modern authentication mechanisms:

🔒 Critical Product Decision: The App does not provide or support custom email/password registration. We exclusively support secure third-party login via Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple. Google Sign-In is also supported on the web version.

We will never ask for, transmit, or store your passwords. All authentication procedures are performed directly on Google's or Apple's secure servers.

2. Common Account and Multi-Provider Linking

To let you manage card data seamlessly across platforms, we use a unified account system:

  • Account Linking: You can log in with Google and then link your Apple account, or log in with Apple and link your Google account.
  • Single Identity ID: Once linked, both Google and Apple identities point to a single Internal User ID, sharing the same card collections, watchlists, and preferences.
  • Unlinking Safety Guard: You can unlink a provider at any time, but the system requires you to retain at least one login method to prevent account lockout.
  • Identification Principle: We **do NOT use Email as the primary unique key** (to prevent issues with Apple's Private Relay email hiding, or mismatching emails between providers). All linking is tied to the unique provider account ID.

3. Data We Collect and How We Use It

When you sign in or link your Google or Apple accounts, we receive limited public profile information:

  • Email Address: Used as your account identifier and for communications (could be a Private Relay address).
  • Display Name: Used to personalize greetings on the App interface.
  • Provider User ID: A non-sensitive unique ID string provided by Google or Apple.

How Data is Used:

  1. User Authentication: To secure your account and access.
  2. Cloud Syncing: To sync your watchlists, favorite cards, and settings, allowing you to access them seamlessly across multiple devices and the web.
  3. Push Notifications: If you enable card price alerts, we use your device push token (generated and managed by Expo Push Service) to deliver notifications. This token is used solely for sending alerts.

4. How Users Sign Out

You can sign out of the App at any time:

  • Tap the Settings tab at the bottom right of the App home screen.
  • In the "Common Account Settings" section, tap the Sign Out button.
  • Upon signing out, cached user profile credentials on your local device will be cleared immediately.

5. Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play requirements, we provide a complete account and data deletion process:

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is irreversible. Once deleted, your Internal User ID, all linked Google/Apple provider associations, and all watchlists and favorites stored in our cloud database will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account:

  1. In-App Self-Service (Recommended): Go to the Settings tab, tap the red Delete Account button, and confirm in the popup dialog. All your account linkings and data in our database will be deleted immediately.
  2. Email Request: If you cannot access the App, send an email request from your registered email address to: dicoge.chen@gmail.com with your User ID. We will process your request manually within 2-3 business days.
