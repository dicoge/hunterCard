Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 8 月 3 日

歡迎使用 HoloHunter（以下簡稱「本 App」或「我們」）。本 App 是一款專為 hololive OFFICIAL CARD GAME (hOCG) 玩家設計的非官方卡牌查價工具。本政策說明本 App 目前實際會收集哪些資料、如何使用與儲存、與哪些第三方服務互動，以及您如何登出與刪除資料。當您使用本 App 時，即表示您同意本政策。

ℹ️ 資料儲存重點： 本 App 沒有自己的雲端帳號資料庫。您的登入帳號資料、收藏、追蹤清單與偏好設定都只儲存在您自己的裝置本機（手機 App 為 AsyncStorage、網頁版為瀏覽器 localStorage），不會同步到我們的伺服器，也不會在裝置之間同步。唯一會離開您裝置的資料是：(1) 掃描卡牌時上傳的卡片影像，(2) 若您在手機上開啟通知，會上傳的推播 Token。詳見下方各節。

1. 登入方式：Google 登入與訪客模式

本 App 不提供自建 Email／密碼註冊，也不會要求、傳輸或儲存您的任何密碼。目前提供兩種使用方式：

  • Google 帳號登入（已啟用）：透過 Google 官方 OAuth 2.0（PKCE）流程進行，身分驗證完全在 Google 端完成。
  • 訪客模式：無須登入即可瀏覽卡表與手動查價。（相機掃描辨識功能需登入後才能使用。）

⚠️ Apple 登入目前尚未開放。 設定頁雖顯示「使用 Apple 帳號登入」按鈕，但目前停用中（需伺服器端驗證 Apple ID Token 才能安全啟用）。點擊後會提示您改用 Google 登入。因此本政策中關於帳號的說明，實際上僅適用於 Google 登入。

2. 登入時我們取得哪些資料，儲存在哪裡

當您使用 Google 登入時，本 App 會向 Google 取得下列您 Google 帳戶的個人資料，並儲存在您的裝置本機（不會上傳到我們的伺服器）：

  • Google 帳號識別碼 (sub)：Google 提供的唯一帳號識別碼，用於辨識您的登入身分。
  • 電子郵件地址 (Email)。
  • 姓名 (name，含 given name / family name)：用於在 App 介面顯示。
  • 個人資料相片網址 (picture)：您的 Google 頭像連結。
  • OAuth 存取權杖與更新權杖 (access token / refresh token) 與到期時間：用於維持您的登入狀態。這些權杖同樣只儲存在您的裝置本機。

以上資料的用途僅限於：讓您登入、在介面顯示您的帳號資訊，以及維持登入狀態。掃描功能設有一個每月次數上限（登入的免費使用者每月 100 次），此計數只儲存在您的裝置本機、非伺服器端配額，訪客則無法使用掃描。本 App 目前沒有可購買的付費訂閱、也不處理任何付款；如未來導入，將於本政策另行揭露。

3. 相機影像與卡牌辨識（會上傳到伺服器並交由 Google Gemini 辨識）

使用掃描功能時，本 App 會以相機取得卡牌影像來辨識卡片。請注意目前的實際流程：

📷 手機 App（iOS / Android）與網頁版皆會上傳影像。 兩個平台在掃描時都會將擷取並前處理後的卡牌影像，傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card；伺服器再將影像轉送給 Google Gemini 視覺辨識模型（Google 的 Generative Language API，generativelanguage.googleapis.com）進行即時判讀。

  • 用途：影像僅用於辨識該張卡片以提供查價，不會用於廣告或建立個人檔案。
  • 內容：影像為卡片畫面，可能含背景，不含您的帳號或身分識別資訊。
  • 保留：本 App 伺服器端不長期儲存影像；影像於傳輸期間交由 Google 依其政策處理。Google 隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. 推播通知與追蹤清單

  • 追蹤清單 (Watchlist)：您加入追蹤的卡牌只儲存在裝置本機，不會上傳到伺服器。
  • 推播 Token（僅手機 App）：若您在手機上允許通知權限，App 會取得一組 Expo 推播 Token，連同平台別（iOS / Android）上傳到我們的伺服器並存放於 Vercel KV。此 Token 以裝置為索引、不與您的 Google 帳號綁定，僅用於未來發送卡牌價格通知。網頁版不會取得或上傳推播 Token。

5. 如何登出

您可以隨時在 App 首頁的「設定」分頁點擊「登出」。登出時，App 會清除裝置本機儲存的登入資料，並向 Google 送出權杖撤銷 (revoke) 請求以撤銷本 App 的授權。

6. 帳號與資料刪除

⚠️ 請注意目前「刪除帳號」的實際範圍。 在「設定」分頁點擊「刪除帳號」並確認後，App 目前的實際行為是：移除儲存在本裝置上的登入帳號紀錄，並將您登出。此操作目前不會：自動向 Google 撤銷授權、刪除伺服器端已上傳的推播 Token，亦不會清除本機的追蹤清單／掃描歷史。（本 App 沒有雲端帳號資料庫，故無雲端帳號資料需要刪除。）

若您想更完整地清除資料，建議：

  1. 撤銷 Google 授權：使用「設定 → 登出」（會向 Google 撤銷本 App 的權杖），或前往您的 Google 帳戶 → 安全性 → 第三方應用程式與服務 移除 HoloHunter 的存取權。
  2. 刪除已上傳的推播 Token：目前尚無 App 內移除入口，如需刪除，請來信 dicoge.chen@gmail.com 告知，我們會於伺服器端移除。
  3. 清除本機資料：於系統設定解除安裝 App（手機）或清除網站資料（網頁）即可移除所有本機儲存的收藏、追蹤清單與設定。

7. 第三方服務與資料安全

  • 傳輸加密：所有離開裝置的資料皆透過 HTTPS 加密傳輸。
  • 不作廣告用途、不販售或分享個資：本 App 不含廣告或分析追蹤 SDK，不會將您的個人資料出售或分享給廣告商。
  • 使用到的第三方處理者：
    • Google：Google 登入 (OAuth)、取得帳號基本資料，以及卡牌辨識所用的 Gemini 視覺模型。
    • Vercel：本 App 後端 API（/api/recognize-card、/api/push/register）與推播 Token 儲存 (Vercel KV) 之託管。
    • Expo：手機推播通知的遞送服務。

Welcome to HoloHunter ("the App" or "we"). HoloHunter is an unofficial price-lookup tool for players of the hololive OFFICIAL CARD GAME (hOCG). This policy describes what data the App actually collects today, how it is used and stored, which third parties it talks to, and how you can sign out and delete data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

ℹ️ Where your data lives: HoloHunter has no cloud account database of its own. Your sign-in account data, collections, watchlist, and preferences are stored only on your own device (AsyncStorage on the mobile App, browser localStorage on the web). They are not synced to our servers and are not synced across devices. The only data that ever leaves your device is: (1) the card image uploaded when you scan a card, and (2) if you enable notifications on the mobile App, your push token. See the sections below.

1. Sign-In: Google Sign-In & Guest Mode

The App does not offer custom email/password registration and never asks for, transmits, or stores any password. There are currently two ways to use it:

  • Google Sign-In (enabled): Performed via Google's official OAuth 2.0 (PKCE) flow; authentication happens entirely on Google's side.
  • Guest Mode: Browse the card list and look up prices manually without signing in. (The camera scan recognition feature requires signing in.)

⚠️ Sign in with Apple is currently disabled. The Settings screen shows a "Sign in with Apple" button, but it is turned off right now (it requires server-side verification of the Apple ID token to enable safely). Tapping it prompts you to use Google Sign-In instead. Accordingly, the account sections of this policy apply in practice to Google Sign-In only.

2. What We Collect at Sign-In, and Where It Is Stored

When you use Google Sign-In, the App retrieves the following personal data from your Google account and stores it on your own device (it is not uploaded to our servers):

  • Google account ID (sub): Google's unique account identifier, used to recognize your sign-in.
  • Email address.
  • Name (name, including given/family name): Shown in the App interface.
  • Profile picture URL (picture): Your Google avatar link.
  • OAuth access token / refresh token and expiry: Used to keep you signed in. These tokens are likewise stored only on your device.

This data is used solely to sign you in, display your account info in the UI, and keep you signed in. The scan feature has a monthly usage limit (100 scans/month for signed-in free users); this count is stored only on your device and is not a server-side quota, and guests cannot scan. The App currently has no purchasable paid subscription and processes no payments; if any are introduced later, this policy will be updated to disclose them.

3. Camera Images & Card Recognition (uploaded and processed by Google Gemini)

When you use the scan feature, the App uses the camera to capture a card image for recognition. Please note the actual flow today:

📷 Both the mobile App (iOS / Android) and the web version upload the image. On both platforms, the captured and pre-processed card image is sent to our server endpoint /api/recognize-card, which then forwards it to Google's Gemini vision model (Google's Generative Language API, generativelanguage.googleapis.com) for real-time reading.

  • Purpose: The image is used only to identify that card for price lookup, never for advertising or profiling.
  • Contents: The image is a picture of the card and may include background; it carries no account or identity information.
  • Retention: Our server does not retain the image long-term; during transit it is processed by Google under its policy. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Push Notifications & Watchlist

  • Watchlist: Cards you add to your watchlist are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to the server.
  • Push token (mobile App only): If you grant notification permission on the mobile App, it obtains an Expo push token and uploads it, together with the platform (iOS / Android), to our server, where it is stored in Vercel KV. The token is keyed by device and is not linked to your Google account; it is only for delivering future card price notifications. The web version does not obtain or upload a push token.

5. How to Sign Out

You can sign out at any time from the Settings tab by tapping Sign Out. On sign-out, the App clears the sign-in data stored on your device and sends a token revocation request to Google to revoke the App's authorization.

6. Account & Data Deletion

⚠️ Please note the actual scope of "Delete Account" today. When you tap Delete Account in Settings and confirm, the App's current behavior is to remove the sign-in account record stored on this device and sign you out. This action currently does not: automatically revoke your Google authorization, delete the push token already uploaded to the server, or clear your local watchlist / scan history. (The App has no cloud account database, so there is no cloud account data to delete.)

If you want a more complete cleanup, we recommend:

  1. Revoke Google authorization: Use "Settings → Sign Out" (which revokes the App's token with Google), or go to your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services and remove HoloHunter's access.
  2. Delete an uploaded push token: There is no in-app removal entry yet; to delete it, email dicoge.chen@gmail.com and we will remove it on the server.
  3. Clear local data: Uninstalling the App (mobile) or clearing site data (web) removes all locally stored collections, watchlist, and settings.

7. Third-Party Services & Data Safety

  • Encryption in transit: All data that leaves your device is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • No advertising use, no selling or sharing personal data: The App contains no advertising or analytics-tracking SDKs and never sells or shares your personal data with advertisers.
  • Third-party processors used:
    • Google: Google Sign-In (OAuth), retrieving basic account info, and the Gemini vision model used for card recognition.
    • Vercel: Hosting for the App's backend APIs (/api/recognize-card, /api/push/register) and push-token storage (Vercel KV).
    • Expo: Delivery service for mobile push notifications.
