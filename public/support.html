💬 常見問題 (FAQ)

Q: 訪客模式 (Guest Mode) 有何功能限制？
訪客模式僅開放「規則導覽」與「手動搜尋卡牌」。為了防止頻寬與伺服器資源濫用，卡牌相機掃描 (OCR) 與 AI 價格趨勢預測 等功能，需要您以 Google 或 Apple 帳號登入後方能使用。
Q: 免費版會員的每月掃描配額 (Quota) 是多少？
（未來正式版行為）凡是登入但未付費訂閱的免費版會員，系統每個月會提供 100 次的卡牌相機掃描額度。當月額度使用完畢後，相機將自動鎖定，直到次月 1 號 (UTC) 自動重置，或者您也可以選擇訂閱升級以立即解除次數限制。

目前版本（本機模擬）：掃描計數僅為本機模擬數值，並非伺服器端配額；清除快取或重裝 App 即會重置，尚未實作真正的每月伺服器端額度控管。
Q: 升級「訂閱版會員 (Subscriber)」有哪些專屬功能？
（未來正式版）訂閱會員可享受以下專屬高級功能：
1. 無限卡牌掃描：完全解除每月 100 張的掃描上限限制。
2. AI 價格與趨勢預測：解鎖獨家的價格走向預測，結合 YT 社群訂閱趨勢、新聞情緒與市場歷史數據綜合演算分析，助您精準掌握卡牌交易的最佳時機。

目前版本（本機模擬）：「升級訂閱」只是在本機把角色切換為訂閱版以示範解鎖效果，不會真的驗證帳號或付款，也未串接 StoreKit / Google Play Billing / Stripe。
Q: 可以同時綁定 Google 與 Apple 帳號嗎？
（未來正式版）HoloHunter 支援「共通帳號」機制。您可以先用 Google 登入，然後在「設定 (Settings)」頁面中點擊「綁定 Apple」完成連結；或是使用 Apple 登入後綁定 Google。綁定後兩者將共享相同的 Internal User ID、雲端收藏與訂閱狀態。

目前版本（本機模擬）：登入與綁定皆為本機模擬，會寫入固定的假帳號並在本機切換狀態，尚未串接真實 OAuth、後端資料庫或雲端同步。
Q: 綁定帳號時顯示「帳號衝突 (Collision)」該怎麼辦？
若您要綁定的 Google 或 Apple 帳號已經關聯到另一個獨立的使用者帳號，系統會偵測到衝突並詢問您：
1. 資料合併 (Merge)：將衝突帳號的卡牌收藏與本帳號合併，並將登入方式轉綁至本帳號。
2. 拒絕並取消：取消綁定，保持現狀。
Q: 刪除帳號會連同我的掃描紀錄跟訂閱一併刪除嗎？
目前版本（本機模擬）：目前並沒有任何雲端帳號或資料。您的所有資料（模擬帳號、掃描計數、收藏清單）都只存在您自己的裝置本機。當您在 App 內點擊「刪除帳號與資料（本機）」後，本機登入狀態與快取會被立即清除並登出——這已完整刪除本 App 目前所持有、關於您的全部資料。此動作無法復原。

未來正式版（串接後端與金流後）：屆時 App 內刪除按鈕會清除本機 Session；若要一併刪除雲端上的 Internal User ID、第三方登入關聯、每月配額計數與收藏清單，並將歷史訂閱交易紀錄去識別化，可透過支援信箱 dicoge.chen@gmail.com 附上 Internal User ID 提交申請，由後端級聯刪除處理並回信通知。（此雲端刪除管道於目前模擬版本尚未實作，因目前並無雲端資料。）
Q: 為什麼不支援一般 Email 密碼註冊？
為了維護您的密碼安全與保障隱私，我們只支援 Google 與 Apple 第三方安全驗證。不收集也不儲存您的個人密碼，所有身分驗證程序均直接在第三方安全端進行。
Q: 我掃描的卡牌照片會被上傳或儲存嗎？
視平台而定：
手機 App（iOS / Android）：文字辨識完全在您的裝置本機進行，卡牌影像不會離開您的裝置，也不會上傳到我們的伺服器。
網頁版：卡牌影像會傳送到我們的伺服器端點，再直接交由第三方 AI 視覺辨識服務 Google Gemini（Google AI Studio 的 Generative Language API）即時判讀。影像僅用於當次辨識，辨識後不作長期儲存，也不會用於廣告或個人化追蹤。詳見隱私權政策。

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What are the restrictions in Guest Mode?
Guest Mode only permits browsing game rules and manually searching cards. To prevent server resources abuse, card scanning (camera OCR) and AI price trend forecasts require you to log in with a Google or Apple account.
Q: What is the monthly scan quota for Free Users?
(Future production behavior) Registered free tier users receive 100 card scans per month. Once you exhaust this quota, the scanning capability locks until the 1st of the next month (UTC), when it resets. Alternatively, you can subscribe to upgrade for unlimited scans.

Current build (local mock): the scan count is only a local mock value, not a server-side quota; clearing cache or reinstalling resets it. Real server-side monthly quota enforcement is not implemented yet.
Q: What do I unlock as a Premium Subscriber?
(Future production) Premium Subscribers get full access to:
1. Unlimited Card Scanning: Scans are completely unlimited.
2. AI Price Trend Forecasts: Unlock predictions using advanced modeling based on YT subscriber stats, sentiment tracking, and historical price variants.

Current build (local mock): "Upgrade to subscription" simply flips your role to subscriber locally to demonstrate the unlock effect. It does not authenticate an account or take payment, and is not connected to StoreKit / Google Play Billing / Stripe.
Q: Can I link both my Google and Apple accounts?
(Future production) HoloHunter supports a "Common Account" mechanism. You can log in with Google first, then go to the Settings page and tap Link Apple, or vice versa. Both methods will share the same Internal User ID and cloud card collections.

Current build (local mock): sign-in and linking are a local mock that writes a fixed fake account and toggles state on-device; real OAuth, a backend database, and cloud sync are not connected yet.
Q: What happens if I get an "Account Collision" warning?
If the Google/Apple account you are linking is already registered, the App will detect the collision and offer two options:
1. Merge Data: Merge card collections/settings of the collided account into your current account and transfer the identity link.
2. Reject & Cancel: Cancel the linking process and keep everything as is.
Q: Does account deletion wipe my scan logs and subscriptions?
Current build (local mock): there is no cloud account or data at this stage — all of your data (mock account, scan count, collections) lives only on your own device. Tapping "Delete Account (Local)" clears your local session and caches immediately and signs you out, which fully deletes everything the App currently holds about you. This action is irreversible.

Future production (after backend & billing): the in-app delete button will clear the local session; to also delete your cloud-stored Internal User ID, linked OAuth identities, scan quota histories, and synced card collections — and to de-identify historical payment records — you will be able to submit a request via our support email dicoge.chen@gmail.com with your Internal User ID, handled by backend cascade deletion. (This cloud deletion channel is not implemented in the current mock build, since no cloud data exists yet.)
Q: Are my scanned card photos uploaded or stored?
It depends on the platform:
Mobile App (iOS / Android): Text recognition runs entirely on your device; the card image never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
Web version: The card image is sent to our server endpoint and processed in real time by the third-party AI vision service Google Gemini (Google AI Studio's Generative Language API, called directly). The image is used only for that single recognition, is not retained long-term, and is never used for advertising or personalized tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
