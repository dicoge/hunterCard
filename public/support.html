💬 常見問題 (FAQ)

Q: 我需要登入才能使用嗎？訪客模式有哪些限制？
不一定。訪客模式無須登入即可瀏覽卡表與手動查價。若要使用相機掃描辨識功能，則需登入。目前可用 Google 帳號登入；在 iOS 上也可使用 Sign in with Apple。
Q: 我可以用 Apple 帳號登入嗎？
可以，在 iOS 上 Sign in with Apple 已啟用，以系統原生的 Apple 登入進行，並由我們的後端驗證 Apple 身分權杖。網頁版的 Apple 登入則由伺服器端功能旗標控管（需 Services ID 與後端密鑰）；未開啟時，Apple 按鈕會完全隱藏。您也可以將 Google 與 Apple 綁定到同一個帳號。
Q: 掃描卡牌有次數限制嗎？
有。已登入的使用者每月可掃描 100 次；訪客無法使用掃描。此次數僅記錄在您的裝置本機，每月自動重置，並非伺服器端配額（清除本機資料或重裝 App 亦會重置）。目前沒有可購買的付費方案，也不處理任何付款。
Q: 我掃描的卡牌照片會被上傳或儲存嗎？
會上傳以進行辨識。手機 App（iOS / Android）與網頁版都會將擷取的卡牌影像傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card，再轉送給 Google Gemini 視覺辨識模型（Google Generative Language API）即時判讀。影像僅用於當次辨識，本 App 伺服器端不作長期儲存，也不會用於廣告或個人化追蹤。詳見隱私權政策。
Q: 我的帳號資料存在哪裡？收藏與追蹤清單會跨裝置同步嗎？
您的帳號身分資料（內部使用者 ID、Google／Apple 的 subject，以及 Email／名稱／頭像快照）採伺服器權威方式，儲存於我們的後端（Vercel KV）；您的裝置僅快取一份快照與登入 session。相對地，您的收藏、追蹤清單與偏好設定只儲存在您使用的那台裝置本機（手機為 AsyncStorage、網頁為瀏覽器 localStorage），不會在裝置之間同步。
Q: 點「刪除帳號」實際會刪除哪些資料？
「刪除帳號」是伺服器權威、fail-closed 的刪除：只有在伺服器確認成功時，才會將您的內部帳號與所有綁定身分（Google／Apple 的 subject、Email／名稱／頭像快照）從我們的資料庫（Vercel KV）永久刪除並清除本裝置 session；若伺服器未能確認，App 不會宣稱已刪除，您仍為登入狀態。

其他須知：
1. Apple 授權撤銷：依 App Store 規範 5.1.1(v)，若帳號綁定了 Apple，刪除時伺服器須先成功撤銷 Apple 授權才會繼續；無法撤銷則拒絕刪除（fail-closed）。（此撤銷持久化仍在完成中，綁定 Apple 的帳號可能暫時收到「尚未開放」並刻意不予刪除。）
2. 刪除範圍：不含匿名推播 Token（請來信 dicoge.chen@gmail.com 移除）與裝置本機資料（收藏、追蹤清單、偏好、掃描歷史）。
3. 清除本機資料：解除安裝 App（手機）或清除網站資料（網頁）即可移除所有本機收藏、追蹤清單與設定。
Q: 為什麼不支援一般 Email 密碼註冊？
為了維護您的密碼安全與保障隱私，本 App 只支援 Google 與 Apple 的第三方安全驗證。我們不收集也不儲存您的密碼；登入時只在裝置取得服務商的 ID Token，交由我們的後端驗證。

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Do I need to sign in? What can I do in Guest Mode?
Not necessarily. Guest Mode lets you browse the card list and look up prices manually without signing in. To use the camera scan recognition feature, you must sign in. You can sign in with a Google account, and on iOS you can also use Sign in with Apple.
Q: Can I sign in with Apple?
Yes — Sign in with Apple is enabled on iOS, using the system-native Apple sign-in, with the Apple identity token verified by our backend. Web Apple sign-in is gated behind a server-side feature flag (it needs a Services ID and a backend secret); while off, the Apple button is hidden entirely. You can also link both Google and Apple to the same account.
Q: Is there a limit on card scanning?
Yes. Signed-in users can scan 100 times per month; guests cannot scan. This count is stored only on your device and resets monthly — it is not a server-side quota (clearing local data or reinstalling also resets it). There is currently no purchasable paid plan, and no payments are processed.
Q: Are my scanned card photos uploaded or stored?
Yes, they are uploaded for recognition. Both the mobile App (iOS / Android) and the web version send the captured card image to our server endpoint /api/recognize-card, which forwards it to Google's Gemini vision model (Google Generative Language API) for real-time reading. The image is used only for that single recognition, is not retained long-term on our server, and is never used for advertising or personalized tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Q: Where is my account data stored? Do my collections and watchlist sync across devices?
Your account identity data (internal user ID, the Google/Apple subject, and email/name/photo snapshots) is server-authoritative and stored in our backend (Vercel KV); your device only caches a snapshot plus the session. By contrast, your collections, watchlist, and preferences are stored only on the device you use (AsyncStorage on mobile, browser localStorage on web) and do not sync across devices.
Q: What does "Delete Account" actually delete?
"Delete Account" is a server-authoritative, fail-closed deletion: only when the server confirms success does it permanently delete your internal account and all linked identities (the Google/Apple subjects and the email/name/photo snapshots) from our database (Vercel KV) and clear the session on this device. If the server cannot confirm, the App does not claim it was deleted and you remain signed in.

Also note:
1. Apple authorization revocation: per App Store guideline 5.1.1(v), if the account has a linked Apple identity, deletion first requires the server to successfully revoke the Apple authorization; if it cannot, deletion is refused (fail-closed). (This revocation persistence is still being finalized, so accounts with a linked Apple identity may temporarily get a "not yet available" result and are intentionally not deleted.)
2. Deletion scope: it does not cover the anonymous push token (email dicoge.chen@gmail.com to remove) or device-local data (collections, watchlist, preferences, scan history).
3. Clear local data: uninstall the App (mobile) or clear site data (web) to remove all local collections, watchlist, and settings.
Q: Why isn't there a regular email/password sign-up?
To protect your password and privacy, the App only supports secure third-party sign-in with Google and Apple. We never collect or store your password; at sign-in the App only obtains the provider's ID token on your device, which our backend verifies.
