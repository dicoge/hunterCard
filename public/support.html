💬 常見問題 (FAQ)

Q: 為何不支援一般密碼註冊？
HoloHunter 旨在提供極簡與安全的體驗。為了避免使用者的帳號密碼外洩風險，我們只提供且支援 Google 與 Apple 第三方快速登入。不自行管理或儲存您的密碼，所有身分驗證直接在第三方安全端進行。
Q: 登入的好處是什麼？
登入後，系統會將您的卡牌收藏與關注價格提醒清單同步至雲端。無論您更換手機，或是在 Web 網頁版上開啟 HoloHunter，您的追蹤清單都能保持一致。
Q: 如何登出帳號？
請前往 App 內右下角的「設定 (Settings)」分頁，在帳號區段中點擊「登出」按鈕。登出後將立即清除裝置上的身分驗證快取。
Q: 如何刪除我的帳號與所有儲存資料？
本 App 提供符合 App Store 規範的自主刪除服務。請前往「設定」分頁，點擊紅色的「刪除帳號與資料」按鈕並確認。此操作會立即且永久清除您在雲端的所有收藏清單、偏好與帳號。若無法登入 App，您亦可寄信至 dicoge.chen@gmail.com，我們將為您手動處理。
Q: 為什麼 App 要求相機權限？
當您使用「掃描卡牌」功能時，App 會請求相機權限，此權限僅用於擷取卡牌影像並進行裝置端的即時 OCR 文字辨識（如卡號、成員名）。我們絕對不會上傳、儲存或分享您的任何照片或相機影像。
Q: 價格資料是即時的嗎？
App 顯示的日圓售價來源為日本知名的卡牌二手交易市場「遊々亭 (yuyu-tei.jp)」。價格資料於每天早上 5:00 AM JST 透過自動化流程爬取並更新至我們的主資料庫中。

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why is there no option to register with an email and password?
HoloHunter is built for maximum simplicity and safety. To completely eliminate the risk of password leaks, we exclusively support Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple. We do not store or manage user passwords; authentication is securely handled by Google and Apple.
Q: What are the benefits of signing in?
Signing in syncs your favorites list and price alerts to our secure cloud. This ensures your tracked cards stay updated across different devices and the web version of HoloHunter.
Q: How do I sign out?
Go to the Settings tab at the bottom right of the App, and tap the Sign Out button in the Account section. This will immediately clear your session cache.
Q: How can I delete my account and all associated data?
We provide a self-service deletion option. Go to the Settings tab, tap the red Delete Account button, and confirm. All cloud watchlists, settings, and profile info will be permanently erased. Alternatively, you can email dicoge.chen@gmail.com, and we will manually delete your data.
Q: Why does the App request camera permissions?
Camera access is required for the Scan Card feature to capture card images and run real-time OCR text recognition on-device. We do not upload, store, or share any camera images or photos.
Q: Are card prices real-time?
JPY prices are sourced from the Japanese card market "Yuyu-tei" (yuyu-tei.jp). Pricing data is scraped and updated automatically every day at 5:00 AM JST in our database.
