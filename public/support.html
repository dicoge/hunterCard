💬 常見問題 (FAQ)

Q: 訪客模式 (Guest Mode) 有何功能限制？
訪客模式僅開放「規則導覽」與「手動搜尋卡牌」。為了防止頻寬與伺服器資源濫用，卡牌相機掃描 (OCR) 與 AI 價格趨勢預測 等功能，需要您以 Google 或 Apple 帳號登入後方能使用。
Q: 免費版會員的每月掃描配額 (Quota) 是多少？
凡是登入但未付費訂閱的免費版會員，每個月會提供 100 次的卡牌相機掃描額度。當月額度使用完畢後，相機將自動鎖定，直到次月 1 號 (UTC) 自動重置；未來訂閱方案上線後，付費會員可立即解除次數限制。

目前版本：掃描計數目前為裝置本機計數（清除快取或重裝 App 會重置）。伺服器端的每月額度控管將於後續版本上線；Google / Apple 登入本身已在後端運作，帳號 sync binding 目前於 Store MVP 版本並未安裝，僅在 feature flag 打開的建置（例如 Web Develop / Staging）才會啟用。
Q: 升級「訂閱版會員 (Subscriber)」有哪些專屬功能？
訂閱會員未來可享受以下專屬高級功能：
1. 無限卡牌掃描：完全解除每月 100 張的掃描上限限制。
2. AI 價格與趨勢預測：解鎖獨家的價格走向預測，結合 YT 社群訂閱趨勢、新聞情緒與市場歷史數據綜合演算分析，助您精準掌握卡牌交易的最佳時機。

目前版本狀態：付費訂閱與 App 內購尚未開放；付費方案上線後將透過 Apple StoreKit 2 / Google Play Billing / Stripe Checkout 依平台處理，並以 Store API 動態載入方案定價，App 內不會硬編碼任何金額。訂閱狀態解鎖以真實付款事件為準，本機不會出現「示範解鎖」路徑。
Q: 可以同時綁定 Google 與 Apple 帳號嗎？
HoloHunter 支援「共通帳號」機制。您可以先用 Google 登入，然後在「設定 (Settings)」頁面中點擊「綁定 Apple」完成連結；或是使用 Apple 登入後綁定 Google。綁定後兩者將共享相同的 Internal User ID；掃描配額與付費訂閱狀態並未包含在 Internal User ID 目前實際共享的範圍內（見上方 banner）。

目前版本狀態：Google / Apple OAuth 與帳號後端已在運作；帳號綁定端點（api/auth/link）與雲端同步端點（api/auth/sync）皆為真實伺服器路徑。Store MVP 上架版本中，sync binding 未安裝，因此不會有實際的 sync 請求；付費訂閱狀態尚未開放。
Q: 綁定帳號時顯示「帳號衝突 (Collision)」該怎麼辦？
若您要綁定的 Google 或 Apple 帳號已經關聯到另一個獨立的使用者帳號，系統會偵測到衝突並詢問您：
1. 資料合併 (Merge)：將衝突帳號的卡牌收藏與本帳號合併，並將登入方式轉綁至本帳號。
2. 拒絕並取消：取消綁定，保持現狀。
Q: 刪除帳號會連同我的掃描紀錄跟訂閱一併刪除嗎？
僅綁定 Google（未同時綁定 Apple）— App 內自助可用：「刪除帳號與資料」會呼叫後端刪除端點（POST /api/auth/delete-account），級聯清除您的 Internal User ID、Google identity 對應以及任何已由 api/auth/sync 儲存的資料，並清除本機 Session 與快取後將您登出。後端不會撤銷您的 Google refresh_token；App 在 Android 版會執行 GoogleSignin.signOut() 清除本機 SDK 快取，其他平台為 no-op。Store MVP 上架版本因未安裝 sync binding，實務上尚未產生 sync 資料需要級聯清除。付費訂閱與 App 內購目前尚未開放，因此暫無交易記錄需要去識別化；付費方案上線後，會一併把交易紀錄依平台既有規範處理。刪除操作為不可逆。

綁定 Apple 的帳號（包含 Apple-only 與 Google+Apple 同時綁定）— App 內尚未提供：Apple 平台的帳號刪除要求後端撤銷登入當下取得的 refresh_token，這需要 refresh_token 加密儲存於後端；目前 api/_lib/apple-token-store.ts 為 non-shipping stub。只要帳號的 linkedProviders 內有任何 Apple identity，端點就會 fail-closed 且不刪除任何資料，回應分兩種：501 apple_revocation_not_configured（伺服器端 APPLE_* 環境變數未設定，getAppleConfig() 回 null）或 501 apple_deletion_not_implemented（Apple 設定完備但 stub token store 沒有可撤銷的 refresh_token）。綁定 Apple 的使用者（含 Google+Apple）請寄信到 dicoge.chen@gmail.com，附上您登入所用的 Email 或 Internal User ID，我們會人工執行相同的級聯刪除並回信通知。

若您無法登入 App 進行刪除（例如已解除安裝），亦可透過同一支援信箱提交申請。
Q: 為什麼不支援一般 Email 密碼註冊？
為了維護您的密碼安全與保障隱私，我們只支援 Google 與 Apple 第三方安全驗證。不收集也不儲存您的個人密碼，所有身分驗證程序均直接在第三方安全端進行。
Q: 我掃描的卡牌照片會被上傳或儲存嗎？
視平台而定：
手機 App（iOS / Android）：文字辨識完全在您的裝置本機進行，卡牌影像不會離開您的裝置，也不會上傳到我們的伺服器。
網頁版：卡牌影像會傳送到我們的伺服器端點，再直接交由第三方 AI 視覺辨識服務 Google Gemini（Google AI Studio 的 Generative Language API）即時判讀。影像僅用於當次辨識，辨識後不作長期儲存，也不會用於廣告或個人化追蹤。詳見隱私權政策。

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What are the restrictions in Guest Mode?
Guest Mode only permits browsing game rules and manually searching cards. To prevent server resources abuse, card scanning (camera OCR) and AI price trend forecasts require you to log in with a Google or Apple account.
Q: What is the monthly scan quota for Free Users?
Registered free tier users receive 100 card scans per month. Once you exhaust this quota, the scanning capability locks until the 1st of the next month (UTC), when it resets. When a paid subscription launches, subscribers will be able to unlock the cap.

Current build: the scan count is currently a per-device counter (clearing cache or reinstalling resets it). Server-side monthly quota enforcement will land in a subsequent build; Google / Apple sign-in itself already runs on the real backend, but the account sync binding is NOT installed under Store MVP — only feature-flag-enabled builds (e.g. Web Develop / Staging) install it today.
Q: What do I unlock as a Premium Subscriber?
Premium Subscribers will get full access to:
1. Unlimited Card Scanning: Scans are completely unlimited.
2. AI Price Trend Forecasts: Unlock predictions using advanced modeling based on YT subscriber stats, sentiment tracking, and historical price variants.

Current build status: paid subscriptions and in-app purchase are not open yet. When a paid plan launches, billing will be routed through Apple StoreKit 2 / Google Play Billing / Stripe Checkout depending on the platform, with plan pricing loaded dynamically through the Store API — the app never hard-codes an amount. Subscription unlock is gated on a real payment event; there is no local "demo unlock" path.
Q: Can I link both my Google and Apple accounts?
HoloHunter supports a "Common Account" mechanism. You can log in with Google first, then go to the Settings page and tap Link Apple, or vice versa. Both methods will share the same Internal User ID; scan quota and paid subscription state are NOT part of what the Internal User ID currently shares (see the banner above).

Current build status: Google / Apple OAuth and the account backend are already live; the linking endpoint (api/auth/link) and the cloud-sync endpoint (api/auth/sync) are real server routes. Store MVP does not install the sync binding, so no actual sync request fires; paid subscription state is not open yet.
Q: What happens if I get an "Account Collision" warning?
If the Google/Apple account you are linking is already registered, the App will detect the collision and offer two options:
1. Merge Data: Merge card collections/settings of the collided account into your current account and transfer the identity link.
2. Reject & Cancel: Cancel the linking process and keep everything as is.
Q: Does account deletion wipe my scan logs and subscriptions?
Google-only accounts (no Apple identity linked) — in-app self-service: "Delete Account" calls the backend delete endpoint (POST /api/auth/delete-account), which cascades removal of your Internal User ID, Google identity mapping, and any cloud-stored data api/auth/sync holds; it then clears your local session + caches and signs you out. The backend does not revoke your Google refresh_token; on Android the App calls GoogleSignin.signOut() to clear the local SDK cache, and other platforms are no-ops. Store MVP does not install the sync binding, so in practice no sync data has been written. Paid subscriptions and in-app purchases are not open yet, so there are no transaction records to de-identify; once paid plans launch, transaction records will be handled per each platform's existing regulations. This action is irreversible.

Any account with Apple linked (Apple-only or Google+Apple combined) — in-app deletion NOT available yet: Apple's platform-deletion contract requires the backend to revoke the sign-in refresh_token, which requires encrypted server-side token storage; today api/_lib/apple-token-store.ts is a non-shipping stub. Whenever an account's linkedProviders contains any Apple identity, the endpoint fails closed and deletes nothing. The exact response is either 501 apple_revocation_not_configured (server-side APPLE_* env vars unset, getAppleConfig() returns null) or 501 apple_deletion_not_implemented (Apple config present but the stub token store holds no refresh_token). Apple-linked users (including Google+Apple): please email dicoge.chen@gmail.com with the email you signed in with or your Internal User ID; we will run the same cascade deletion by hand and reply to confirm.

If you cannot sign in to the App to perform the deletion (for example, you already uninstalled), you can submit a request via the same support email.
Q: Are my scanned card photos uploaded or stored?
It depends on the platform:
Mobile App (iOS / Android): Text recognition runs entirely on your device; the card image never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
Web version: The card image is sent to our server endpoint and processed in real time by the third-party AI vision service Google Gemini (Google AI Studio's Generative Language API, called directly). The image is used only for that single recognition, is not retained long-term, and is never used for advertising or personalized tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
