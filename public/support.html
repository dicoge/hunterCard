💬 常見問題 (FAQ)

Q: 我需要登入才能使用嗎？訪客模式有哪些限制？
不一定。訪客模式無須登入即可瀏覽卡表與手動查價。若要使用相機掃描辨識功能，則需以 Google 帳號登入。目前僅開放 Google 登入。
Q: 為什麼「使用 Apple 帳號登入」按鈕沒有反應？
Apple 登入目前尚未開放。安全啟用 Apple 登入需要在伺服器端驗證 Apple ID Token，此部分尚未建置，因此按鈕暫時停用，點擊後會提示您改用 Google 登入。請先使用 Google 帳號登入。
Q: 掃描卡牌有次數限制嗎？
有。以 Google 登入的使用者，每月可掃描 100 次；訪客無法使用掃描。此次數僅記錄在您的裝置本機，每月自動重置，並非伺服器端配額（清除本機資料或重裝 App 亦會重置）。目前沒有可購買的付費方案，也不處理任何付款。
Q: 我掃描的卡牌照片會被上傳或儲存嗎？
會上傳以進行辨識。手機 App（iOS / Android）與網頁版都會將擷取的卡牌影像傳送到我們的伺服器端點 /api/recognize-card，再轉送給 Google Gemini 視覺辨識模型（Google Generative Language API）即時判讀。影像僅用於當次辨識，本 App 伺服器端不作長期儲存，也不會用於廣告或個人化追蹤。詳見隱私權政策。
Q: 我的收藏與追蹤清單會跨裝置同步嗎？
不會。本 App 沒有雲端帳號資料庫，您的登入帳號資料、收藏、追蹤清單與偏好設定只儲存在您使用的那台裝置本機（手機為 AsyncStorage、網頁為瀏覽器 localStorage），不會在裝置之間同步。
Q: 點「刪除帳號」實際會刪除哪些資料？
目前「刪除帳號」的實際行為是：移除儲存在本裝置上的登入帳號紀錄並將您登出。它目前不會自動向 Google 撤銷授權、刪除伺服器端已上傳的推播 Token，或清除本機的追蹤清單／掃描歷史。

若要更完整地清除：
1. 撤銷 Google 授權：使用「設定 → 登出」（會嘗試向 Google 撤銷本 App 授權，屬盡力而為、可能未成功），或到 Google 帳戶安全性設定移除 HoloHunter（此方式一定生效）。
2. 刪除已上傳的推播 Token：來信 dicoge.chen@gmail.com，我們會於伺服器端移除。
3. 清除本機資料：解除安裝 App（手機）或清除網站資料（網頁）即可移除所有本機收藏、追蹤清單與設定。
Q: 為什麼不支援一般 Email 密碼註冊？
為了維護您的密碼安全與保障隱私，本 App 只支援 Google 第三方安全驗證（Apple 登入未來開放）。我們不收集也不儲存您的密碼，身分驗證程序均直接在 Google 端進行。

💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Do I need to sign in? What can I do in Guest Mode?
Not necessarily. Guest Mode lets you browse the card list and look up prices manually without signing in. To use the camera scan recognition feature, you must sign in with a Google account. Only Google Sign-In is available right now.
Q: Why does the "Sign in with Apple" button do nothing?
Sign in with Apple is not available yet. Enabling it safely requires server-side verification of the Apple ID token, which has not been built, so the button is temporarily disabled and prompts you to use Google instead. Please use Google Sign-In for now.
Q: Is there a limit on card scanning?
Yes. Users signed in with Google can scan 100 times per month; guests cannot scan. This count is stored only on your device and resets monthly — it is not a server-side quota (clearing local data or reinstalling also resets it). There is currently no purchasable paid plan, and no payments are processed.
Q: Are my scanned card photos uploaded or stored?
Yes, they are uploaded for recognition. Both the mobile App (iOS / Android) and the web version send the captured card image to our server endpoint /api/recognize-card, which forwards it to Google's Gemini vision model (Google Generative Language API) for real-time reading. The image is used only for that single recognition, is not retained long-term on our server, and is never used for advertising or personalized tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Q: Do my collections and watchlist sync across devices?
No. The App has no cloud account database. Your sign-in account data, collections, watchlist, and preferences are stored only on the device you use (AsyncStorage on mobile, browser localStorage on web) and do not sync across devices.
Q: What does "Delete Account" actually delete?
Today, "Delete Account" removes the sign-in account record stored on this device and signs you out. It currently does not automatically revoke your Google authorization, delete the push token already uploaded to the server, or clear your local watchlist / scan history.

For a more complete cleanup:
1. Revoke Google authorization: use "Settings → Sign Out" (which attempts to revoke the App's token with Google on a best-effort basis and may not succeed), or remove HoloHunter in your Google Account security settings (this always works).
2. Delete an uploaded push token: email dicoge.chen@gmail.com and we will remove it on the server.
3. Clear local data: uninstall the App (mobile) or clear site data (web) to remove all local collections, watchlist, and settings.
Q: Why isn't there a regular email/password sign-up?
To protect your password and privacy, the App only supports secure third-party sign-in with Google (Apple coming later). We never collect or store your password; authentication happens entirely on Google's side.
